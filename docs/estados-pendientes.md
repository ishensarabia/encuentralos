# Estados pendientes

Covered with contacts read on an official page (2026-10-01): Ciudad de México, Estado de México,
Jalisco, Michoacán, Chiapas.

The other 27 states are served by the national channels (CNB phone, WhatsApp and online report;
art. 81 lets anyone report to the CNB, any Comisión Local or the Ministerio Público). To add one:
create `content/states/<id>.json` like `michoacan.json`, with the commission's contacts **read on
the state's official page**, and add the page to `content/sources.json`.

Rule: never copy a phone number from a news article or a search summary. They disagreed with the
official pages (Michoacán, Tamaulipas) when checked.
